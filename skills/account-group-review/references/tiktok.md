# TikTok

Research checked 2026-10-05 (America/Chicago). See `tt-links`, `tt-entitlements`,
`tt-verification`, and `tt-copy` in `research.json`.

Store native account type, business entitlement and ViewPrinter `connectVariant`
separately. TikTok's May 2026 business documentation names **General**, **Advanced
Access**, and **Verified Business Account**; the latter was previously called
Registered Business Account. Advanced Access permits editing some business assets
without necessarily displaying them publicly. A business OAuth connection alone
does not establish verification or a displayed website link.

Consumer help reports website links at 1,000 followers or a Registered Business
Account. The newer entitlement table reserves public business-profile decoration
and organic conversion anchors for Verified Business Accounts. These sources have
different scopes and terminology; preserve both, and check the actual Website field
and public clickability. Do not present either as a universal promise. Social-account
links are a separate feature with regional availability.

For business verification, the May 2026 instructions specify age 18+, appropriate
business documents for the operating region, matching legal information and owner
verification. Settings and privacy → Account → Business verification is the documented
path; the in-app path may vary. Business verification differs from the blue badge.
Store only status and evidence references, never submitted document contents.

The live ViewPrinter schema reports an 80-unit bio and 24-unit username limit, and
2,200 UTF-16 units for API post captions. Keep titles/link fields separate. A copied
URL in plain text is not proof of a clickable Website field. Prepare a bio that still
makes sense if a link is unavailable; use a link-specific CTA only after verification.

When there is no working external profile link, give an actionable alternative. For
an app brand, an explicit “Search [exact app name] in your app store” CTA is clearer
than the app name alone. Keep the conditional website destination and setup path in
feature requirements, rather than showing an unconditional Website field in the
current copy kit. Record “no link present” separately from unknown link eligibility.

October 5 follow-up: the indexed official Account types article explicitly says
Personal Accounts with at least 1,000 followers can post a website link. This is a
native personal-profile rule; the May 2026 Business Suite entitlement table describes
business features and must not be used alone to deny the personal route. Consumer
instructions: Profile → Edit profile → Links → Add; look for Website. Confirm the
actual field and saved public display. Native type can be checked in Settings and
privacy → Account; a business connector label still does not establish it.

Use a square avatar master and small circular preview. For vertical video, 1080×1920
is the observed delivery recommendation. Right-side controls and bottom captions vary;
record any production inset as a project guide, not a platform guarantee. Inspect
cover/grid crop independently. Commercial audio access and link eligibility are
separate checks; a profile audit should not silently switch account types for either.
